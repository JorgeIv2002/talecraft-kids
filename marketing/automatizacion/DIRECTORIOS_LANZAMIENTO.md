## 3. Indie Hackers (Show IH)
* **Title:** How we built a calm, non-addictive interactive story app for kids (and why parents love it)
* **Body:**
  > "Overstimulation in digital media is one of the biggest concerns for modern parents. With TaleCraft Kids, we set out to build the anti-TikTok for bedtime:
  > - Horizontal curated library rails (inspired by Vooks and calm Scandinavian storybooks).
  > - Real human studio narrators with soothing tempos and bedtime lullaby music.
  > - Interactive decision forks that reward kindness, courage, and perseverance.
  > - A Star Shop where children unlock animal companions without ads or predatory microtransactions.
  > Built with Next.js PWA and full offline-first audio streaming. Check it out and let us know what you think!"
